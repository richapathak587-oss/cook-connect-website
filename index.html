Who we are

Cook Collective is a 6500sq. feet shared kitchen serving
your small business commissary kitchen needs.

Our goal is to provide space for a community of like minded
entrepreneurs.where we can problem-solve,support and
grow together.

We strive to create a global collective of diverse food
business to supply the world one great,meal at a time!

What we offer




Cook Collective has 8 customizable preparation
  stations,fully licensed and equipped.



We offer Station Rentals by the shift and monthly storage options for dry.cold and frozen items.

7.5 hour Shifts. AM Shift [8:00AM-3:30PM] PM Shift[4:00PM-11:30PM]Overnight Shift[12:00AM-7:30PM]

Our stations are ample,refrigerated and have plenty of storage.

Memberships start as low as 2 shifts per month.

Choose a plan from 1 to 7 shifts a week. Prices start from $195 per shift.
